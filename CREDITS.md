# Credits

Photographs and other work by others that the game uses, kept here until the About screen shows them.

## Birds

The birds are photographs from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org), cut out of their backgrounds, resized and, for the 3D birds, painted onto their models (`tools/bird-sprites.py`, `tools/bird-models.py`). Each tool writes the credits of the photographs it used to `assets/birds/birds.json` and `assets/birds/models.json`.
